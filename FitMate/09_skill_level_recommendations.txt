Contoso Sports – Skill Level Recommendations (Detailed Guide)

Overview:
At Contoso Sports, we group our products into three skill levels: Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced. This helps customers find gear that matches where they are in their sporting journey. Recommending the right level is important — gear that is too advanced can be uncomfortable or unnecessary, while gear that is too basic can hold back someone ready to improve.

---

HOW TO IDENTIFY A CUSTOMER'S SKILL LEVEL:

When helping a customer choose by skill level, ask simple questions like:
- "How often do you play or train?"
- "Have you played this sport before?"
- "Are you looking for something to get started, or are you training regularly?"
- "Is this for casual use or competitive play?"

Use their answers to guide them to the right level. Most customers will fall naturally into one group.

BEGINNER LEVEL:

Who is a Beginner?
- Someone trying a sport for the first time
- A casual player who plays occasionally (once a week or less)
- Someone returning to a sport after a long break
- Children just starting to learn a sport
- People who want comfortable, easy-to-use gear without complications

What Beginner Gear Focuses On:
- Maximum comfort and ease of use
- Forgiving designs that do not punish mistakes
- Durability to withstand learning-phase wear
- Accessible pricing
- Simple features without overwhelming technology

INTERMEDIATE LEVEL:

Who is Intermediate?
- Someone who plays or trains regularly (2-4 times per week)
- A club-level player or regular gym-goer
- Someone who understands the basics and wants to improve
- A fitness enthusiast with consistent training habits
- Someone ready for better-performing gear

What Intermediate Gear Focuses On:
- Balance between comfort and performance
- Improved materials and construction
- Better support, responsiveness, and durability
- Features that help the user improve
- Mid-range pricing with good value

ADVANCED LEVEL:

Who is Advanced?
- Competitive athletes and serious sports enthusiasts
- Someone who trains 4+ times per week
- League or tournament-level players
- People who want maximum performance from their gear
- Experienced users who know what features matter to them

What Advanced Gear Focuses On:
- Maximum performance and responsiveness
- Lightweight construction for speed and efficiency
- Premium materials and construction quality
- Sport-specific technology and features
- Higher price point reflecting premium quality

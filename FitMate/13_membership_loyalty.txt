Contoso Sports – Membership and Loyalty Program

Note: All membership details are simulated for demo and testing purposes.

---

PROGRAM NAME: Contoso Sports Club

OVERVIEW:
Contoso Sports Club is our free loyalty program. When you sign up, you earn points on every purchase, get access to member-only deals, and receive personalized recommendations. There is no cost to join, and you start earning benefits immediately.

---

HOW TO JOIN:

In-Store:
1. Visit any Contoso Sports store
2. Ask a team member to sign you up
3. Provide your name, email address, and phone number
4. You will receive a digital membership card via email
5. Your card can be stored in your phone's wallet app

Online (Simulated):
1. Visit www.contososports.example.com
2. Click "Join the Club" on the homepage
3. Fill in your details (name, email, phone number)
4. Create a password for your account
5. Your membership is activated immediately

Requirements:
- Must be 16 years or older
- Valid email address required
- One membership per person
- Free to join — no annual fee

---

MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS:

1. Earn Points on Every Purchase:
   - Earn 1 point for every dollar spent
   - Points are added automatically when you scan your membership card or log in online
   - Points appear in your account within 24 hours of purchase

2. Redeem Points for Rewards:
   - 100 points = $5 reward voucher
   - 200 points = $12 reward voucher (bonus value)
   - 500 points = $35 reward voucher (best value)
   - Vouchers can be used on any purchase (no minimum spend)
   - Vouchers expire 6 months after issue

3. Member-Only Discounts:
   - Exclusive member pricing on selected products each month
   - Early access to seasonal sales (24 hours before general public)
   - Birthday discount: 15% off one purchase during your birthday month

4. Member-Only Events:
   - Invitations to product launch events
   - Priority registration for in-store workshops
   - Early access to limited-edition products

5. Personalized Recommendations:
   - Based on your purchase history and preferences
   - Seasonal gear suggestions for your favorite sports
   - New product alerts in your preferred categories

6. Digital Receipt Storage:
   - All purchases are stored in your membership account
   - No need to keep paper receipts
   - Makes returns and exchanges easier

---

MEMBERSHIP TIERS:

Contoso Sports Club has three tiers based on annual spending:

1. Bronze (Default):
   - Automatic when you join
   - Earn 1 point per dollar
   - Standard member benefits
   - No minimum spend required

2. Silver (Spend $200+ in a year):
   - Earn 1.5 points per dollar
   - All Bronze benefits plus:
   - Free gift wrapping year-round
   - Exclusive Silver member sale events (2 per year)
   - Priority access to workshops

3. Gold (Spend $500+ in a year):
   - Earn 2 points per dollar
   - All Silver benefits plus:
   - Free alteration service for apparel
   - 20% birthday discount (instead of 15%)
   - Invitation to VIP store events
   - Dedicated customer service line

Tier Renewal:
- Tiers are evaluated annually based on your spending in the previous 12 months
- If your spending qualifies for a higher tier, you are upgraded automatically
- If your spending drops below the threshold, you move to the appropriate tier at renewal
- You never lose your accumulated points when changing tiers

---

FREQUENTLY ASKED MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS:

Q: Is there a membership fee?
A: No. Contoso Sports Club is completely free to join and maintain.

Q: Can I earn points on sale items?
A: Yes, you earn points on all purchases including sale and clearance items.

Q: Do my points expire?
A: Points are valid for 12 months from the date they are earned. If unused, they expire after 12 months.

Q: Can I use my membership at any store?
A: Yes, your membership works at all Contoso Sports store locations and online.

Q: I forgot to scan my card during a purchase. Can I still get points?
A: Yes. Bring your receipt to any store within 14 days and a team member can add the points to your account.

Q: Can I share my membership with family?
A: Each membership is individual. However, family members can each sign up for their own free membership.

Q: How do I check my points balance?
A: You can check your balance by logging in to the website, through the app, or by asking a team member in-store.

Q: Can I use reward vouchers with other promotions?
A: Reward vouchers can be used on most purchases. However, they cannot be combined with other vouchers or coupons. Member-only discounts and birthday discounts are separate from reward vouchers.

Q: What happens if I return an item I earned points on?
A: The points earned from that purchase will be deducted from your account when the return is processed.

HOW THE ASSISTANT SHOULD HANDLE MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS:

- Explain benefits enthusiastically — the program is genuinely helpful
- Encourage sign-up for non-members: "It is free and you start earning points right away!"
- For existing members asking about points or tier: suggest checking online or in-store
- The assistant cannot look up specific account balances, points, or tier status
- If a customer has a membership issue, offer to connect them with customer service

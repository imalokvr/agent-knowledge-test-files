Contoso Sports – Returns, Exchanges, and Warranty Policy

Note: All policies are simulated for demo and testing purposes.

---

RETURN POLICY:

Standard Return Window:
- You can return most products within 30 days of purchase
- The product must be unused, unworn, and in its original packaging
- Tags must still be attached
- You will need your receipt or proof of purchase (digital receipt is accepted)

Conditions for Returns:
- The item must be in the same condition as when you bought it
- Shoes must be unworn (no signs of outdoor use on the sole)
- Apparel must be unwashed and unworn (trying on at home is fine)
- Equipment must be unused and in original packaging
- Swimwear must have the hygiene liner intact (if applicable)

Items That Cannot Be Returned:
- Swimwear with the hygiene liner removed
- Customized or personalized items
- Items marked as final sale or clearance
- Used cricket bats (due to knocking-in and usage marks)
- Opened food or nutrition products (energy bars, gels)

How to Return In-Store:
1. Bring the item to any Contoso Sports store
2. Bring your receipt or digital proof of purchase
3. A team member will inspect the item
4. If approved, you will receive a refund to your original payment method
5. Refund processing takes 3-5 business days for card payments
6. Cash purchases are refunded in cash immediately

How to Return Online Purchases (Simulated):
1. Log in to your account on the Contoso Sports website
2. Go to your order history and select the item to return
3. Print the return label
4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging
5. Drop it off at the designated courier location
6. Refund is processed within 5-7 business days after the item is received

---

EXCHANGE POLICY:

Standard Exchange:
- You can exchange a product for a different size, color, or equivalent product
- Exchange window: 30 days from purchase
- The item must be in unused, unworn condition with tags attached

How to Exchange In-Store:
1. Bring the item and your receipt to any Contoso Sports store
2. Choose the replacement item (same product in a different size or color)
3. If the replacement is a higher price, you pay the difference
4. If the replacement is a lower price, you receive the difference as store credit

Size Exchange for Online Orders (Simulated):
1. Contact customer service or use the website return portal
2. Select "exchange" and specify the new size or color
3. Ship the original item back
4. The replacement is shipped once the return is received
5. No extra shipping cost for size exchanges within the same product

When to Recommend an Exchange:
- Customer bought the wrong size (common with shoes and apparel)
- Customer wants a different color
- Customer received a gift and needs a different size
- The product has a manufacturing defect (see warranty section)

---

WARRANTY POLICY:

Standard Warranty:
- All Contoso Sports products come with a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects
- This covers defects in materials, construction, and workmanship
- This does NOT cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or accidental damage

What Is Covered:
- Stitching coming undone within normal use
- Sole separating from the shoe
- Zippers failing under normal use
- Fabric tearing without external cause
- Equipment breaking under normal use conditions

What Is NOT Covered:
- Normal wear and tear (sole wearing down from running, fabric fading from washing)
- Damage from misuse (using road running shoes for hiking, for example)
- Accidental damage (dropping equipment, stepping on goggles)
- Damage from improper washing or care
- Cosmetic issues that do not affect function (minor color variations, small marks)

How to Make a Warranty Claim:
1. Bring the product to any Contoso Sports store
2. Bring your receipt or proof of purchase
3. A team member will inspect the product to determine if the defect is covered
4. If approved, you will receive a replacement or store credit
5. The store does not offer cash refunds for warranty claims — only replacement or credit

Extended Warranty (Optional):
- Available at the time of purchase for selected products (shoes and equipment)
- Extends the warranty from 6 months to 12 months
- Small additional cost at checkout
- Same coverage as standard warranty, but for a longer period

---

TIPS FOR CUSTOMERS:

Keeping Your Receipt:
- Always keep your receipt. A photo of the receipt on your phone works too.
- If you signed up for the loyalty program, your purchases are stored in the system.

Care Instructions:
- Follow the care label on apparel to maintain warranty coverage
- Store shoes in a cool, dry place
- Clean equipment after use to prevent premature wear
- Do not machine wash items unless the label says it is safe

When to Contact Customer Service:
- If you are unsure whether your item qualifies for a return, exchange, or warranty claim
- If you have lost your receipt but have a bank statement showing the purchase
- If you have a complaint about a product or experience

IN-STORE SERVICES:

1. Product Fitting and Sizing:
   - Available for: Shoes, cycling helmets, cricket pads, hiking boots
   - How it works: Our in-store advisors help you find the right size and fit. For shoes, we measure your foot length and width. For helmets, we measure head circumference.
   - Cost: Free
   - Appointment needed: No, walk in any time
   - Tip: Wear the socks or base layer you plan to use with the product for the most accurate fitting.

2. Shoe Fitting Service:
   - What we do: Measure foot length, width, and arch type. Recommend shoes based on your sport, foot shape, and any comfort preferences.
   - Available for: Running shoes, football boots, hiking boots, gym shoes
   - Cost: Free
   - Time: Usually takes about 10-15 minutes
   - Tip: Come in the afternoon — feet naturally swell during the day, so afternoon fitting gives you the most comfortable match.

3. Cricket Bat Knocking-In Service:
   - What we do: Prepare new cricket bats for use by compressing the willow fibers with a mallet. This hardens the surface and prevents cracking.
   - Available for: English willow bats (Kashmir willow bats require less knocking-in)
   - Cost: Small service fee applies
   - Time: Usually 3-5 business days
   - Note: New bats should always be knocked in before use. Using an un-knocked bat risks cracking the willow.

4. Racket Restringing Service:
   - What we do: Replace worn or broken strings on badminton, tennis, and squash rackets.
   - Cost: Service fee plus string cost
   - Time: Usually same day or next day
   - Note: Contoso Sports primarily sells racket accessories. Full racket restringing depends on store location.

5. Bike Accessory Fitting:
   - What we do: Help fit helmets, lights, bottle cages, and saddle bags to your bike
   - Cost: Free when purchased in-store
   - Time: Usually 10-15 minutes
   - Note: We do not sell or service bicycles, but we can help you set up accessories you buy from us.

6. Workshops and Events (Seasonal):
   - What: Occasionally we host beginner workshops — running technique, cricket batting basics, hiking preparation talks
   - When: Seasonal, usually weekends. Check in-store notice boards or social media.
   - Cost: Usually free or small registration fee
   - Registration: In-store or via the store's social media page
   - Note: Workshop availability varies by store and season.

7. Gift Wrapping:
   - Available for any purchase
   - Cost: Free
   - When: All year, but especially popular during holiday season
   - Just ask any team member at checkout.
